Privacy policy

Last updated: 04/10/2026 (date)

runsesh is a small app for step competitions between friends. This page explains, in plain language, what data runsesh collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs runsesh

Antonio Doblas - student from Malaga, Spain

What we collect

  • Email address: to create your account, log you in and send account emails (confirmation, password reset).
  • Display name: the name your party sees.
  • Avatar: the character you design (body color, hair, clothes, accessories).
  • Time zone: so your "today" matches your local day.
  • Daily step totals: one number per day, plus where it came from (typed in by you, or read from Health Connect).

Your password goes straight from the app to our login provider (Supabase Auth), which stores only a secure hash of it. runsesh itself never stores, logs or displays your password.

Android: Health Connect

On Android, the runsesh app reads your steps from Health Connect only after you grant permission, and only the step count permission is requested. The app reads your daily totals and sends only those totals to our server. It does not read or send individual walks, locations, heart rate or any other health data. You can remove the permission at any time in the Health Connect settings.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to show rankings and competitions among the members of your party.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use tracking or analytics tools.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and login service). West EU(ireland).

Other people can only see what the app shows them: in a later version, members of your party will see your display name, avatar and daily step totals.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When an account is deleted, its profile and step totals are deleted with it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and all data. Until in-app deletion is available, send the request to the contact below. Expected response time: 1-2 working days.
