Privacy policy

Last updated: 06/10/2026

runsesh is a small app for step competitions between friends. This page explains, in plain language, what data runsesh collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs runsesh

Antonio Doblas - student from Malaga, Spain

What we collect

  • Email address: to create your account, log you in and send account emails (confirmation, password reset).
  • Display name: the name your party and friends see.
  • Usertag (@name): a unique name others use to find you and send you a friend invite. One is created for you; you can change it.
  • Friends: who you invited or accepted as a friend, and when.
  • Memories (only if you post them): photos of your day, up to 3 per day, with the date. Before upload the app re-encodes each photo, which removes hidden data such as GPS location and camera details.
  • Avatar: the character you design (body color, hair, clothes, accessories).
  • Time zone: so your "today" matches your local day.
  • Daily step totals: one number per day, plus where it came from (typed in by you, sent from Health Connect on Android, or sent by an Apple Shortcut on iPhone).
  • Phone tokens (only if you connect a phone): for each personal token you create, a scrambled copy (hash) of it, whether it is for Android or iPhone, when it was created and when it last sent data. We never keep the token itself.

Your password goes straight from the app to our login provider (Supabase Auth), which stores only a secure hash of it. runsesh itself never stores, logs or displays your password.

Android: Health Connect (third-party app)

runsesh has no Android app of its own. If you want your Android steps sent automatically, you choose to install Life Dashboard Companion, an independent open-source app that is not made or controlled by runsesh. That app reads Health Connect on your phone under its own privacy terms, with the permissions you grant it. Our set-up guide asks you to grant it only the Steps permission.

The app sends its data to runsesh with a personal token that you create in runsesh. From what it sends, runsesh keeps only your daily step totals (today and the last 3 days). Anything else in the message (for example minute-by-minute step records or other data types) is ignored: it is not stored and not logged.

iPhone: Apple Shortcut

On iPhone, an Apple Shortcut that you build yourself reads your step total from Apple Health and sends only that daily total to runsesh, with a personal token. Apple Health data stays on your phone otherwise.

Personal tokens

A token only lets a phone send your own daily steps. It cannot read your data or log in. It is shown to you once; we store only a scrambled copy (hash), so we cannot show it again. You can revoke a token at any time (Profile → Health connect, or runsesh on iPhone); it stops working immediately. Tokens are deleted with your account.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to show rankings and competitions among the members of your party.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use tracking or analytics tools.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and login service). West EU(ireland).

Other people can only see what the app shows them:

  • Any signed-in player can find your usertag by searching (they see only the usertag, nothing else) and send you a friend invite.
  • Your friends and the members of your party see your display name, usertag, avatar, when you joined and your daily step totals; party members also see your time zone and competition results.
  • Your memories are visible to you, your friends and your party members, until you delete them.
  • Nobody else can see your steps, and nobody can see your tokens.

You can remove a friend at any time; they lose access immediately.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When an account is deleted, its profile and step totals are deleted with it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and all data. Until in-app deletion is available, send the request to the contact below. Expected response time: 1-2 working days.
