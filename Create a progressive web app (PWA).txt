The architecture of the first page goes as follows:

- And underneath that a "COMPETITION" label in red over a circus type drawing. That should rank all the party members based on the competition rules applied by the party leader.

The functionality of the app goes as follows:
the app tracks the steps of all party members using a connection to google health in case of android and apple health in case of apple .
Their position in the track slider is determined by their number of steps. The daily ranking also ranks the party members by how many steps they have on that specific day.

The COMPETITION ranking ranks members based on the set up chosen by the party leader. First the party leader choses a start date and a finish date. And then choses the ranking option, if by number of days won, by points where a day won gives 3 points second place 2 points and third place 1 point. Or by total number of steps in the month.

in the second page as said before when clicking on the avatar depiction a new page appears where you can customize your avatar. The avatar is a simple dumy. The player can chose the color of the avatar using a RGB wheel. They can choose the hair clothes and accesories from many options of each different subcategory. By default the icon is a close up of the avatar of the user but they can upload a photo to use as their icon or pfp in other words.

When the user clicks on "party settings" different options appear:
-manage party members: where they can invite new members and kick players from the party
-party rules: here they can choose how steps are counted if simply by number of steps or by applying a bonus if a steps counted was made in an increase of elevation

When the user clicks on "Competition" a page appear following the circus like style mentioned before.
if no competition already exists a button showing "create a Competition" if they click on it they will be prompted to setup the intial date and finish date (this can be left open) they will be asked about the rules of the competition from the ones mentioned before. And whether to add a reward to the current leader of the competition (which is a a set of golden wearables made of golden glasses golden running vest, golden boots, a jewelled golden crown and golden avatar color)

when the user clicks on login/log out they can create an account if they dont have one, login into an existing account or log out of their account.

when the user clicks on account they can see their account details wich include name, email, password (which should be pixelated)

when the user clicks on health connect they should be able to manage their connection to their corresponding health app and permissions.
